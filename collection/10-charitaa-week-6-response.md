*Responding to Jonathan Lee. Charitaa’s note on what was said:* "Taste becomes useful when it is informed by the product and the people using it"

> This was a super interesting perspective on this because we often hear that since AI can do most things for us, our main job is going to be to use "our good taste" to determine the best outcome and let the machine "handle the rest". But this made me think about how the concept of good taste is also going to evolve with the rest of the industry. Less about having a good visual eye and more about knowing what is actually building and what problems are worth solving. Taste becomes truly useful when it is informed by the context within which it must exist! Example: When you make 100 different prototypes using AI, knowing which versions actually help the target user would be my new definition of having "good taste".

— Charitaa, Week 6, after class
