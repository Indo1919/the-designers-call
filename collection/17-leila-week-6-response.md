*Responding to Jonathan Lee. Leila’s note on what was said:* The industry of AI is still being defined, and pricing and access to the technologies will change over time. The point of giving full access right now is to get the general public comfortable with the concept and to get more information from them. However, even with AI, people will always want the direct access to sources and won't hand off all their work.

> I think this is a very interesting take, because it is true that the general public has access to so many AI technologies right now, and that may become more limited in the future, or need to be paid for. However either way, with AI, humans should take control of the machine rather than the other way around, and choose selectively what we are handing off versus what we are doing ourselves.
