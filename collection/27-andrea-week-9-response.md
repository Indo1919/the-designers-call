*Responding to Simrin. Andrea’s note on what was said:* Art is basically a mix of things that already exist. We take inspiration from other people’s work and ideas then remix them in our own way to create something new. As a painter, she does the work herself, but someone who doesn’t know how to paint might use AI to create the painting they want.

> I think this idea is interesting when we relate it to AI, since AI also combines patterns and influences from existing work to generate something new. It raises questions about originality and how we differentiate human creativity from AI. Charita also mentioned that if AI can replicate an artist’s work so closely, what makes us say that human art is better than AI-generated art?
