> In the words often attributed to Marshall McLuhan (but more properly ascribed to Winston Churchill): 'we shape our tools, and thereafter our tools shape us." We are the technology of our tools: they shape and form us. Our tools have agency, and thus a claim upon the more-than-human world as well.

Bridle, James. Ways of Being: Animals, Plants, Machines: The Search for a Planetary Intelligence. New York: Farrar, Straus and Giroux, 2022, 18.
