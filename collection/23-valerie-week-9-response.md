*Responding to Juliette. Valerie’s note on what was said:* An important trait in intelligent people is their ability to tolerate being stupid for a more extended period of time. If you can stand feeling stupid for eight hours a day and not go crazy, you could become a rocket scientist. They get bored at knowing what's going on, and jump at opportunities to not know anything.

> I think this is a statement that rings true now more than ever. The essence of generative AI is to reduce the time it feels like an idiot by making the duration of the task as short as possible. If you create a 4000 word essay with a 100 word prompt, you didn't have to risk feeling like an idiot for another 3900 words. The opportunity for growth was robbed from you. The mark of intelligence is not the ability to produce/reproduce a product; Lord knows we don't need more student essays or vibe coded websites. Intelligence and taste grows through active authorship, not just through passive, ego-free creation.

— Valerie, Week 9, after class
