*Responding to Andrew. Crystal’s note on what was said:* Asked whether we’re complicit when we use AI to make creative work considering it uses other people's work (ex. all existing Figma files ever) to train on and reference when generating new work.

> I think we are complicit, but it’s hard to feel that way now that AI has become such a normal part of almost everyone work or even in day to day life. When ChatGPT first came out I was very much against it, and as every following agent or assistant came out and more widely used I became a bit more open, and now AI is built into so many tools and everyone uses it that the responsibility feels far removed, even though it’s still there. I think that’s what makes the question important and hard to answer the more normal something becomes, the easier it is to stop questioning the ethics around it since now its seeming okay and "normal"

— Crystal, Week 9, after class
