*Responding to Jonathan Lee. Andrea’s note on what was said:* He talked about finding a balance between having enough ownership over our work and making sure we don’t feel like AI is doing the work for us.

> I think that to be a good designer, we need to maintain control over our work while using AI as a tool rather than letting it make the decisions for us. For example, Jonathan Lee used AI for his presentation slides, but he gave it direction based on his previous work and told it what kind of design to create. In that sense, he was still the one making the creative decisions and using AI to execute or develop his ideas, rather than blindly accepting whatever AI produced.

— Andrea, Week 6, after class
